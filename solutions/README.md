# 🔑 Solutions and Answer Key

![Type](https://img.shields.io/badge/Type-Solutions-2da44e?style=flat-square) ![Modules](https://img.shields.io/badge/Modules-26-1f6feb?style=flat-square) [![Course Home](https://img.shields.io/badge/⬅%20Course%20Home-555?style=flat-square)](../README.md)

Worked answers for every module's quiz and its three labs.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Attempt each lab before opening its solution. Each answer includes the
> **failure mode** the lab is designed to provoke — reproducing that error
> yourself is where the learning is. Reading the finished YAML teaches you what
> the answer looks like, not why the alternatives do not work.

## How each solutions file is organised

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Quiz Answers | The correct option plus *why the distractors are wrong* — the distractors encode the common misconceptions. |
| Lab 1 — Beginner | Complete working YAML, collapsed behind a `<details>` block. |
| Lab 2 — Intermediate | As above, plus the wiring that is easy to get silently wrong. |
| Lab 3 — Challenge | As above, plus the design reasoning the lab asks you to record. |

Every lab answer carries three fixed parts:

- **Why this works** — the mechanism, not a restatement of the code.
- **Verify the failure mode** — the exact error to reproduce, so you can confirm
  you understand the constraint rather than having pattern-matched around it.
- **Common wrong answer** — the plausible attempt that fails, and why.
